CRM & Business Process Automation

End-to-end workflow automation for lead processing, CRM record management, and consultant operations.

Automation Engineer
2024 – Present
Make.com, Zoho CRM, Strive CRM
Professional Work
Project details are shared in general terms to respect client and business confidentiality. No private data, credentials, or internal metrics are disclosed.

Project Overview

This is an ongoing professional engagement focused on designing, building, and maintaining automated business workflows that connect lead sources, CRM platforms, and operational tools into a coherent system.

The work spans the full lifecycle of a lead — from initial capture through a Facebook Ads campaign or Typeform event registration, through automated qualification and record creation in Zoho CRM or Strive CRM, to consultant round-robin assignment and ongoing data integrity.

Scope of Work

  • Lead capture automation: Processing incoming leads from Facebook Ads and routing them into CRM systems with field mapping and deduplication.
  • Event registration workflows: Typeform submissions triggering automated CRM records, Zoom attendance tracking, and event-name standardization.
  • Consultant assignment: Round-robin logic distributing new leads to available consultants based on configurable rules.
  • CRM migration: Moving and normalizing records between CRM platforms with consistent data structure and naming conventions.
  • Workflow QA: Testing, debugging, and monitoring automations to catch failures, data mismatches, or broken triggers before they affect business operations.
  • Google Sheets integration: Using structured spreadsheets as lookup tables, staging areas, and reporting outputs within multi-step Make.com scenarios.

Tools & Technologies

Make.com Zoho CRM Strive CRM Google Sheets Facebook Ads Typeform Zoom

Challenges & Approach

The primary challenge is maintaining data consistency across multiple CRM platforms and data sources that were not originally designed to work together. This requires careful field mapping, error handling in automation scenarios, and ongoing monitoring to catch edge cases.

Event-name standardization is a recurring task — ensuring that leads from different sources, campaigns, and time periods are consistently tagged so reporting remains accurate and useful.

Workflow troubleshooting often involves tracing failures across multiple modules in Make.com to identify where data transforms incorrectly or where API connections time out under load.

Full-Stack Developer

Project Overview

Mechayaki is a digital platform designed for street food vendors to manage customer queues, process pre-orders, and handle menu operations online. The system allows customers to browse menus, place orders ahead of time, and track their queue position — reducing physical congestion and improving the overall dining experience.

Technical Stack

  • Frontend: React 19 with Vite 7 for fast builds and modern bundling. The UI handles menu browsing, order placement, and queue status display.
  • Backend: Spring Boot 3.5.9 running on Java 17, providing RESTful API endpoints for order management, queue logic, and menu administration.
  • Database: PostgreSQL 16 for persistent storage of orders, menu items, and queue state.
  • Infrastructure: Docker for containerized development and deployment, ensuring consistent environments across machines.
