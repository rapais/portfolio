About the Project

A university social responsibility initiative conducted on Pangkor Island, Malaysia. The project combined community outreach with environmental conservation — contributing to the restoration of a local mosque and participating in coastal sea turtle conservation efforts.

Activities

  • Mosque restoration and community upkeep
  • Coastal sea turtle conservation and habitat awareness
  • Community engagement with local residents
  • Team coordination and leadership

What did we do?

As the Leader of this project, I coordinated a university-wide social responsibility initiative involving students from various majors. Our goal was to contribute to community well-being and environmental sustainability through two key initiatives:


🏛 Cleaning Places of Worship

Location: Al-Khairiah Mosque, Pangkor Island,

Activities: Interior & exterior cleaning, community awareness campaigns,

Objective: Promoting hygiene, comfort, and public responsibility.


🏝 Turtle Conservation – Planting & Cleanup Activities

Location: Kampung Penyu, Pulau Pangkor,

Activities: Coastal habitat restoration, beach cleanup, turtle awareness programs,

Objective: Engaging in hands-on conservation efforts for marine biodiversity.


These initiatives brought students together to make a real impact while fostering leadership, teamwork, and environmental awareness.
